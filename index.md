I obtained my PhD at <a href="https://graphics.tudelft.nl/">CGV</a>, TU Delft (Netherlands), with my research conducted at the intersection of Computer Vision and Computer Graphics. I explored image synthesis and 3D animation, focusing on 3D controllability to enable easy interaction with 3D representations and images.

During my PhD, I was fortunate enough to join <a href="https://arvr.google.com/">Google Zurich</a> (Switzerland) for a research internship.
I obtained a M.Sc. degree with honours in <a href="http://ipcv.eu/">Computer Vision and Image Processing</a>, jointly coordinated by the Autonomous University of Madrid (Spain), University of Bordeaux (France), and Pázmány Péter Catholic University (Hungary) under the Erasmus Mundus initiative. I wrote my Master's thesis at the <a href="https://vcg.seas.harvard.edu/">Visual Computing Group</a> at Harvard University (USA). Before this, I obtained a B.Sc. with distinction at the University of Hamburg (Germany) in <a href="https://www.inf.uni-hamburg.de/en/inst/ab/hci.html">Human-Computer Interaction</a>. Next to my studies, I worked as a student software developer at various companies. For more details check my <a href="/assets/pdf/CV_Lukas_Uzolas.pdf">CV</a>.
