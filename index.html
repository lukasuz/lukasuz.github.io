I obtained my PhD at CGV, TU Delft (Netherlands), with my research conducted at the intersection of Computer Vision and Computer Graphics. I explored image synthesis and 3D animation, focusing on 3D controllability to enable easy interaction with 3D representations and images.

During my PhD, I was fortunate enough to join Google Zurich (Switzerland) for a research internship. I obtained a M.Sc. degree with honours in Computer Vision and Image Processing, jointly coordinated by the Autonomous University of Madrid (Spain), University of Bordeaux (France), and Pázmány Péter Catholic University (Hungary) under the Erasmus Mundus initiative. I wrote my Master’s thesis at the Visual Computing Group at Harvard University (USA). Before this, I obtained a B.Sc. with distinction at the University of Hamburg (Germany) in Human-Computer Interaction. Next to my studies, I worked as a student software developer at various companies. For more details check my CV.
